## 3. Touch is not complete

The owner cannot use the sysc-shell bar by touch under sysc-compositor (reported
2026-11-07). Tiles focus by touch, and apps that ask for touch (Chromium, the
probe) get it; everything else does not.

- **The bar and the other shell surfaces** (`sysc-2260`). sysc-shell binds only
  `wl_pointer` (`/home/nomadx/sysc-shell/internal/platform/wayland/client.go:537`)
  and never `wl_touch`. The compositor delivers a finger only to a client that
  asked for touch, so every tap on the bar is dropped: the archThink journal
  logged 56 `touch down dropped: the client did not ask for touch` in the 24 hours
  to 2026-11-07, against 84 deliveries. Niri 26.04 does the same; its
  `on_touch_down` sends native touch only, with no pointer emulation. The
  Niri-consistent fix is in sysc-shell: bind `wl_touch` when the seat advertises
  touch and turn down/up into activation, motion into drags and scrolls. The
  alternative, compositor-side pointer emulation for clients without `wl_touch`,
  departs from Niri and needs the owner's decision before anyone builds it. Ask
  the owner which, then do the work in the sysc-shell repository, and accept it
  on archThink by tapping bar items and using a panel.
- **Compositor-owned UI takes no touch**: the overview, the window switcher, the
  screenshot UI, the colour picker, the exit dialog and the hotkey overlay only
  block a finger while they are open. A tap on a tab indicator does not switch
  tabs either. Niri's touch-down drives its own UI (`on_touch_down` routes the
  screenshot UI and the MRU switcher); port that.
- **Touch neither wakes the monitors nor resets idle** (`sysc-2254`, P1, opened
  2026-11-06), together with pointer, scroll and gesture input.
- **Multi-finger touchscreen gestures** are not implemented, and
  `input.touch.calibration-matrix` still warns. See the touchscreen plan's open list.

The touchscreen plan on `main` (`docs/plans/2026-11-03-touchscreen-input.md`)
still reads as if touch were finished; correct its "What is still open" section
when this work starts.
